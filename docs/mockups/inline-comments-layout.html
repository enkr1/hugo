Understanding Ba Zi: Five Elements

Mar 20, 2026 8 min read 142 views

The five elements — Add commentMetal (金), Water (水), Wood (木), Fire (火), and Earth (土) — form the foundation of Ba Zi analysis. Each element interacts with others through cycles of generation and control.

In the generation cycle, Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth (ash), Earth bears Metal, Metal collects Water (condensation), and Water nourishes Wood. This productive cycle ensures balance and harmony across all domains of life.

// Five element cycle relationships const elements = { '金': { generates: '水', controls: '木' }, '水': { generates: '木', controls: '火' }, '木': { generates: '火', controls: '土' }, '火': { generates: '土', controls: '金' }, '土': { generates: '金', controls: '水' }, };

The control cycle provides checks and balances. Wood controls Earth (roots hold soil), Earth controls Water (dam), Water controls Fire, Fire controls Metal (smelting), and Metal controls Wood (axe). Without control, generation runs unchecked.

Understanding these cycles allows practitioners to identify imbalances in a person's chart. A chart heavy in Fire but lacking Water suggests someone who is passionate but may burn out without deliberate rest and reflection.

The interplay extends beyond individual elements. When we examine the full eight characters (四柱八字), we see how elements interact across the four pillars — Year, Month, Day, and Hour — creating a rich tapestry of influences that shape personality, career aptitude, and relationship dynamics.
