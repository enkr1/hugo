Same loop as `errors.md` for NUS finals: log the miss, name the pattern, redo cold later. The goal is a bit better every week, not a rewrite of how I sound.

**Rules of this log**
- Max **3 items per week**, logged during `/journal-weekly` after Highlights land.
- **Repeats matter more than new entries.** A pattern hit three weeks running is the only real signal; a one-off is noise.
- Voice is never a defect. Lowercase, `u`/`ur`, ellipsis chains, self-address, run-on rhythm and image-making calques are style. Only misfires get logged.

## 2026-07-20 — Week 76

**1. P4 — `make` + bare infinitive**
- Wrote: "it just made me thinking so much that"
- Fix: "it just made me **think** so much that"
- Rule: `make` / `let` / `have` + person + **bare** verb, never `-ing`. Same family: let them brainstorm, have him wait.

**2. P3 — calque misfire (mixed construction)**
- Wrote: "especially seeing how kimi k3 performance is like"
- Fix: "seeing **what** kimi k3 performance is like" (or "seeing how kimi k3 **performs**")
- Rule: pick one frame. `how X performs` **or** `what X is like`. Blending them is the 直译 of 「看看 X 表现如何」.

**3. P10 — announcing the metaphor instead of starting it**
- Wrote: "u could understand it like u're paying to eat in a restaurant where u have a professional chef to cook u ur favourite dish every time"
- Fix: "it's like paying to eat at the same restaurant every night. the chef learns ur favourite dish."
- Rule: 「你可以理解成 / 就好比」 opens a Chinese frame that English doesn't need. Don't translate 理解 as "understand" here. `it's like` or `think of it like` and then **start the image immediately** — the reader will work out it's a metaphor without being told.
- Two further gains worth noticing: cutting "professional" (every restaurant chef is; the adjective the category already implies is always dead weight), and splitting into two sentences so "the chef **learns** ur favourite dish" lands on its own. `learns` instead of `cooks` is what connects the metaphor to Karp's "are you keeping the data".

*Also observed this week but not drilled (swapped out to keep the 3-item cap honest): P5, "paying to company" → "paying the company". One-off preposition fix, low transfer.*

**Standing homework (P1/P2), not counted against the 3:** the next time a `just ... so ... [adjective]` frame appears, delete the adjective and put a verb there. "i just felt so excited and happy" → what did the excitement make me *do*?

## 2026-07-29 — Week 77

**All three items are repeats. Zero new patterns.** Second week of the log, and every miss landed on a row that already existed. The surface area is small and it is the same three doors swinging.

**1. P3 — mixed construction (hit 2, two weeks running)**
- Wrote: "the more i am sure about that i love teaching"
- Fix: "the more **sure i am that** i love teaching"
- Rule: `sure about X` **or** `sure that X`. Never welded together. Identical shape to last week's "how kimi k3 performance is like": two valid frames blended into one invalid sentence. When a phrase feels like it needs both a preposition and a `that`, one of them is wrong.

**2. P6 — relative pronoun dropped while it is the subject (hit 2, two weeks running)**
- Wrote: "did a lot of research on the areas got myself curious"
- Fix: "the areas **that** got **me** curious"
- Rule: drop `that` only when it is the *object* ("the book [that] i read"). Here the areas are doing the making-curious, so `that` is the subject and has to stay. Second half of the fix: `got myself curious` → `got me curious`. Reflexive says I did it to myself; the areas did it to me.

**3. P5 — dropped article (hit 2)**
- Wrote: "grateful for having such relaxing week"
- Fix: "such **a** relaxing week"
- Rule: `such` + adjective + singular countable noun always takes `a`/`an`. The half of P5 that fired this week is the article, not the preposition.

**Not logged, deliberately.** The closing line "building GB is sth i building and having so much fun!!!" carries a missing `'m`, and stays exactly as written. It was the sentence that closed the entry and it went in verbatim by his call. Also left alone: "thats" (dropped apostrophe, voice), "went back jb" (dropped preposition, local idiom), "+" for "and", the ellipsis chain, and the run-on rhythm in the first paragraph.

**Craft note, not a pattern.** The entry originally closed on "grateful for having such a relaxing week", a feeling, and the fourth consecutive entry to end on gratitude. The evidence said the opposite of relaxing: 342 commits on Gym Buddy, the heaviest week of the last five (75 / 108 / 35 / 217 / 342). Naming that contradiction produced the real close, in his own words, unedited. The lesson is not "avoid gratitude endings", it is that the strongest close was already sitting in the gap between what he wrote and what he did.

## 2026-08-04 — Week 78

**1. P5 — dropped article (hit 3, THIRD week running — the only three-week pattern in the register)**
- Wrote: "its just hard for audience to erase"
- Fix: "hard for **the** audience to erase"
- Rule: a singular countable role-noun always carries its article. Three weeks, three different nouns, same door: "paying to **company**" (W76), "such relaxing **week**" (W77), "for **audience**" (W78). The noun changes every week, which is why it never feels like the same mistake. It is.

**2. P8 — number agreement (hit 2, and three instances inside one entry)**
- Wrote: "this mod is one of the useful one" / "im not a movie goers" / "i dont watch movie often"
- Fix: "one of the most useful **ones**" / "not a movie **goer**" / "i dont watch **movies** often"
- Rule: `one of the ...` always ends plural (you are picking one out of many). `a` never sits in front of a plural. A bare countable noun needs either a plural or an article. Three different surface forms, one underlying miss: the noun's number is being decided by the Chinese frame (量词 does that work in Chinese; English makes the noun itself carry it).

**3. P11 — reflexive `myself` where `me` belongs (hit 2, two weeks running; new row, split out of W77's P6 item)**
- Wrote: "from watching odyssey got myself wondering was it CGI/AI?"
- Fix: "watching odyssey got **me** wondering, was it CGI/AI?" (dropping "from" also gives the sentence its subject back)
- Rule: `myself` only when I act on me ("i told myself"). When a thing does it to me, plain `me`. Identical shape to last week's "got myself curious" → "got me curious". Two weeks running means the W77 correction didn't transfer; drill this one cold.

*Also observed, swapped out to keep the 3-item cap honest:* "the principles & concepts i have been **doing** at work" (P2, overworked generic verb; "applying" or "using" is the precise one), and "quite funny to holand and zendaya appear" (missing perception verb: "funny to **see** them appear"). Mechanical typos flagged in chat, not patterns: "gettting", "holand".

*Left alone as voice:* "frens", "goated", "im"/"dont"/"its", the "hmm. weird." fragment pair, "hahaha", the ellipsis close on "A-...", every run-on.

**Craft note.** The close ("cheers for many more trips and adventures with my frens in the future!") is a toast, and it works: concrete, forward-moving, earned by the paragraph above it. Fifth gratitude-adjacent close in a row, but this is the first one where the week's actual centre of gravity (the trip) and the close agree, so nothing to name. The stronger craft gap this week is in paragraph one: "principles and concepts" appears twice in one sentence and "concepts" a third time in the next; the second mention could be "them" or "the why behind the work" and the paragraph would stop circling.
