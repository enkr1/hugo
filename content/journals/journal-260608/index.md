last week, i made a big decision to actually start posting more on xhs. as teaching and sharing is always something i love doing, i decided not to procrastinate, to act on it. this is a big one for me, as it has been in the bottom of my heart for quite a while that i just constantly think of it but never act on it.

a few days after posting them, i got more views & reach all of a sudden! and i actually got a few people seriously reaching out, wanting to sign up for my coaching plan! i just felt so excited and happy.
