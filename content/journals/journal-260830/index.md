## ✨ Highlights of the Week

since last week i've been putting a lot effort into "promoting" my product and im so happy currently i have 2 real users who are actively using my product, ofc they are my friends, but actually i contacted 20+ of my frens. 2 stayed. at this point im questioning my direction but i dont see the point in giving up as im literally using this product myself, it solved my own problem!

it made me come to a realisation that sometimes a product definition of good or bad aint defined by you; but by the market needs. till now, slowly seeing the needs, might be 1 out of 20 who actually have this problem, so in the world of people who take gym seriously, lets say 1,000,000, could be 10,000 needing it, and.... its not a small number either!

so it got me thinking about the direction of how i can add on more bonus features such that alongside recording sets and reps, you get to interact with people in the app as a public space too! and i named it Lobby!

wonderful week ngl, everyday im just using the product i build for my own issue, just find it so lucky to be in this era...

im just so lucky

more to go, hopefully get 100 DAU this EOY!!!!!
