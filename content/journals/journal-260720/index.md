## ✨ Highlights of the Week

was another week of seeing how the current big companies are fighting over their LLMs.

{{< quote author="Alex Karp" source="CNBC Squawk Box, 1 July 2026" url="https://www.mediaite.com/media/tv/you-sound-pretty-angry-cnbc-anchor-left-stunned-as-palantir-ceo-blows-his-stack-during-wild-interview/" >}}
Are you keeping the data? Are you going to enter our business?
{{< /quote >}}

lately i've been realising that we are actually paying the company when we are contributing our data to them. the idea is kinda ridiculous. it's like paying to eat at the same restaurant every night. the chef learns ur favourite dish, and becomes someone who understands u the most. eventually u will never know if the chef would turn ur dishes into their dishes in the restaurant, u'll never know. seeing more and more companies trying to give tokens as a reward for developers to "be creative"... it just made me think so much that

they are basically doing what i do with my subagents, letting them brainstorm some ideas and come back with the best one, in short... but seeing how they do it officially and legally like that and eventually take all the credit is just insane.

it's got me thinking, is it really a win-win? while ur data is far more precious, u're still paying to give out ur data. perhaps, having LLMs running locally would really be a direction slowly. especially seeing what kimi k3 performance is like.
