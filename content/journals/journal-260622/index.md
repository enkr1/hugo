## ✨ Highlights of the Week

but man, what a weekend, attended [lioncity beatbox championship](https://555beatboxsingapore.com/), was so immersed myself into it, participants from thailand, hongkong, malaysia and judges from malaysia and france 🔥 every time i went beatbox event, i look for something that will light up the love feeling in me again. and this time, Stitch (malaysia) sang bohemian rhapsody polyphonic, and i got goosebumps.

he was one of the best improver since 2019 we went Werewolf beatbox championship in indonesia, i didn't pass the wildcard tho, but i could still rewind every moment of it, especially the stage he owned and performed "[i'll see you tonight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIo3_y8hfGY)". 🥺 his voice and arrangement changed how i see beatboxing, and the possibility of doing it.

i don't like to say that, but, days after days from working, from school, my love towards beatboxing, fading? i tried to find it back tho.. like i mention here [back in this blog]({{< ref "why-do-i-love-beatboxing" >}}) but im seeing it now as im so close, im just one step away to find my own style, especially as someone who has been listening to [Colaps](https://www.instagram.com/colapsbbx/) since i started beatboxing. the first time i saw him was a shoutout he did for [Pepouni](https://x.com/pepounisbx). Colaps was just a solid beatboxer back then, but a few years later he showed up at GBB wildcard with a complete new style, sharing a stage with [dlow](https://www.instagram.com/dlowmusic/). that change, that is not just a finding style, that is basically willingness to explore, to accept, to try different things that might not be suitable. back to Stitch, he had a unique sound from the start and good voice naturally, but he didn't stop exploring diff combinations, after the competition, off stage, he was talking to another participant,  i caught that he was sharing that he would come up with 10 diff variations for a single drop just to pick the best one.

that is actually the spirit i'm missing, jinghui, stop sticking with what u know, do the 10-variations thing, stop settling for the first drop that works.

try exploring, jinghui.

i got this.
